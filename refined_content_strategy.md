## Core Promise (Hero Message)
**"Prevent burnout before it steals your sleep, focus, and relationships."**

This is the north star. Every page, every section, every CTA must reinforce this promise.

---

## The Prevention Framework (PEACE + StoryBrand)

### The Problem (What Users Experience)
- Stress creeping up silently
- Sleep getting worse
- Focus slipping away
- Relationships suffering
- By the time they realize it's serious, it's too late

### The External Problem
- Burnout is invisible until it's catastrophic
- Traditional therapy waits for crisis
- No one is watching the early warning signs
- Sleep, focus, and relationships deteriorate gradually

### The Internal Problem
- "Am I losing control?"
- "Why can't I sleep anymore?"
- "I can't focus on what matters"
- "I'm letting down the people I care about"

### The Philosophical Problem
- Mental wellness shouldn't require a crisis to get help
- Prevention is better than cure
- Early intervention changes everything

---

## The Solution: Dr Nur AI (Your Personal Mental Health Coach)

### What Makes It Different
1. **Detects Early** - Uses wearable data to spot stress BEFORE it becomes burnout
2. **Intervenes Early** - Personalized coaching at the first sign of trouble
3. **Prevents Crisis** - Keeps you balanced so burnout never happens

### The Three-Step Prevention Journey

**Step 1: Detect**
- Apple Watch monitors your biometrics in real-time
- AI analyzes heart rate, sleep, activity patterns
- Early warning signs are caught BEFORE you feel the crisis

**Step 2: Coach**
- Personalized AI coach meets you with a micro-plan
- Clinically-backed strategies for your specific situation
- 2-7 minute sessions that fit your life

**Step 3: Prevent**
- You follow the plan and feel the difference
- Sleep improves, focus returns, relationships strengthen
- Burnout never happens because you caught it early
