1. **Early Detection Saves Lives**: Users appreciated that Dr Nur AI caught burnout early, before it became a crisis.

3. **Data-Driven Insights**: Apple Watch data provided concrete evidence of stress patterns, making the problem real and actionable.

4. **Personalized Coaching Works**: Each user found different coaches most helpful, showing the value of personalization.

5. **Ripple Effects**: Improvements in mental health led to improvements in work, relationships, and overall life satisfaction.

6. **Accessibility**: The free plan was enough to get started; the Proactive plan provided comprehensive support.

7. **24/7 Support**: Users appreciated having a coach available anytime, without judgment or waiting for appointments.
