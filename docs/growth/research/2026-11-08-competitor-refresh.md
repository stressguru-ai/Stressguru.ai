# Competitor Refresh

**Dated:** November 8, 2026

**Headspace.** Headspace remains publicly available through its official app page, with a 14-day trial, browser access, and Apple and Android download paths. Its current positioning spans meditation, sleep, expert-led and CBT-oriented programs, and Ebb, an AI companion, with links to care-adjacent options. No verified change from the October 24 baseline was established because the page is undated and provides no dated release marker. [Primary source: Headspace official app page](https://www.headspace.com/app)

**Calm.** Calm’s official plans page remains publicly accessible and presents a seven-day trial, a Calm for Life purchase option, and Premium content covering meditation, stress and anxiety, sleep, music, Sleep Stories, classes, and movement. No verified substantive change from the October 24 baseline was established; the undated page does not support a historical comparison of plan or trial details. [Primary source: Calm official plans page](https://www.calm.com/freetrial/plans)

**Ash.** Ash is currently presented on its official homepage as an AI mental-health and emotional-support product with download links, including a US Apple App Store path, and 24/7 support positioning for stress, anxiety, relationships, and everyday challenges. The homepage is marked published November 5, 2026, but that date does not establish a product-positioning or availability change from October 24. The page also states that Ash is not for crisis use. [Primary source: Ash official homepage](https://www.talktoash.com/)

**Wysa.** Wysa remains publicly available for individuals through its official site and app, positioned as 24/7, anonymous, private, secure AI self-help with more than 200 digital tools and optional in-app wellbeing-coach access. No verified change from the October 24 baseline was established; the page is undated, so its current feature and wording details cannot be dated as newly introduced. The official page also states that Wysa is not for emergencies or clinical advice. [Primary source: Wysa official individuals page](https://www.wysa.com/for-individuals)

**Youper.** Youper’s official homepage remains reachable, but it states that the service was discontinued on September 30, 2026. No newer dated status change was found relative to the October 24 baseline. The residual page describes chatbot, mood-tracking, reflection, journaling, safety, and crisis-resource information, but a reachable informational page does not prove backend or app availability. [Primary source: Youper official homepage](https://www.youper.ai)

**Woebot.** Woebot’s consumer app remains retired: the official FAQ says new accounts cannot be created, previous accounts cannot be accessed, transcript requests closed July 15, 2025, and account data was anonymized July 31, 2025. No verified change in availability or public consumer positioning from the October 24 baseline was established. [Primary source: Woebot official FAQ](https://woebothealth.com/faq/)
