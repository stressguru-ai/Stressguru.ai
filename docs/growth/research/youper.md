# Youper research for StressGuru.ai

**As of:** October 24, 2026
**Author:** Manus AI

## Commercial availability and identity

Youper is **not commercially available**. The official `youper.ai` homepage says that services were discontinued on September 30, 2026. Its data-erasure page says that all user accounts, chat histories, and personal information have been permanently deleted from servers and backups. The terms identify the operator as Youper, Inc. and say the company was winding down. Do not treat the residual website as an app landing page, a sign-up path, or a source of live product features. [1] [2] [3]

The supplied domain is the correct official Youper identity. Ash is a separate product from Slingshot AI, not a Youper rebrand or successor. Slingshot announced Ash's public launch in July 2025, and the current Ash site carries Slingshot AI copyright. [5] [6]

Woebot should also not be presented as a current direct-to-consumer alternative without fresh access verification. Woebot Health's current homepage describes an organization serving people and companies but provides no consumer app download or individual enrollment path. A dated 2025 report says Woebot Health announced retirement of its app for June 30, 2025. [7] [8]

## Product category and historical positioning

Historically, Youper was a consumer, self-guided AI emotional-wellbeing or digital mental-health chatbot. Its official terms describe conversations for mood tracking, emotional reflection, and journaling. The 2021 peer-reviewed study characterized it as a fully automated mobile AI-therapy intervention for anxiety and depression among paying users, but the study was longitudinal and observational, not a randomized trial. [3] [4]

The prior product was not a crisis service, was not medical advice, and was not FDA-cleared or approved, according to its official disclosures. That historical category should not be conflated with an active clinical therapy product. [1] [3]

## Current public stress, work, and sleep content

The current official Youper web presence contains discontinuation, safety, terms, privacy, and data-deletion material rather than an active public education hub. A direct official-site check and focused web-search queries for `Youper stress sleep work`, `site:youper.ai stress sleep work`, and `Youper work stress sleep content` did not surface a current official Youper stress, work, or sleep article. This is a point-in-time visibility observation, not evidence that any phrase is unowned, has a particular search volume or difficulty, or is absent from every web index.

No AI-search citation or answer-engine visibility claim is made here because no answer engine was tested. The search evidence above is limited to an actual web-search query check and the live official pages.

## Narrow content-gap hypotheses for U.S. professional women 30-55

These are evidence-linked hypotheses for StressGuru.ai, not claims that Youper "owns" or fails to own a keyword. Each needs normal editorial, medical, and audience validation before publication.

1. **Question:** How can I stop job uncertainty from turning into bedtime rumination?

   **Competing URL:** [https://www.youper.ai](https://www.youper.ai)

   **Evidence:** APA's 2025 U.S. worker survey found that 54% said job insecurity significantly affected their stress. Workers concerned about job loss were more likely to report sleep and relationship problems. The official Youper site is discontinued and the focused query check found no current official work-plus-sleep page. [9] [1]

   **Opportunity:** Publish a tightly scoped, non-diagnostic guide for women managing a professional role: an after-work “uncertainty containment” routine, a short job-search or worry window, a boundary for news and inbox exposure, a sleep-protective wind-down, and an escalation box for persistent insomnia or severe distress. Test it against reader feedback and on-page engagement, rather than asserting demand from volume estimates.

   **Status:** Hypothesis to validate.

2. **Question:** What can I do in the 30 minutes between finishing work and starting family care when I have no energy left?

   **Competing URL:** [https://www.youper.ai](https://www.youper.ai)

   **Evidence:** CDC reports that about one in five U.S. adults are caregivers; in its 2021-2022 sample, 59.2% of caregivers were women. Caregivers had worse results than noncaregivers on 13 of 19 health indicators, including more frequent mental distress and lifetime depression. The closest published age bands show substantial caregiving participation across ages 30-59, a reasonable but imperfect proxy for the proposed 30-55 audience. [11] The current Youper site has no live content pathway for this combined work-caregiving transition. [1]

   **Opportunity:** Create a practical transition guide for working caregivers: a two-minute decompression cue, a realistic handoff or arrival ritual, one small request for support, and a plan for days when the routine breaks. Frame it as everyday coping and workload management, not treatment for caregiver depression or burnout.

   **Status:** Hypothesis to validate.

3. **Question:** Why does a stressful workday follow me into sleep, and what is a realistic first step tonight?

   **Competing URL:** [https://www.youper.ai](https://www.youper.ai)

   **Evidence:** The U.S. Office on Women's Health lists work deadlines among common stressors, identifies trouble sleeping as a possible effect of chronic stress, and notes that long-term stress can worsen mood and anxiety problems for women. [10] Youper's accessible first-party pages have shifted to shutdown and safety notices, and no current official sleep resource appeared in the focused query check. [1]

   **Opportunity:** Build a plain-language stress-to-sleep explainer with a single evening experiment, such as a work shutdown note and a brief body-calming practice. Include clear guidance to seek clinical evaluation for ongoing insomnia, pregnancy-related concerns, medication questions, or safety concerns. Avoid promises of better sleep or claims that one routine works for every reader.

   **Status:** Hypothesis to validate.

## Implications for StressGuru.ai

The central competitive fact is not a feature comparison: Youper has exited the market. The useful content opportunity is continuity and specificity for professional women facing the connected pressures of work uncertainty, evening caregiving, stress, and sleep. Keep any resulting pages educational, precise about limits, and designed around a named moment of need rather than broad claims of AI-therapy superiority.
