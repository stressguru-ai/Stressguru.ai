# Competitor map: StressGuru.ai

**As of:** October 24, 2026
**Scope:** U.S. professional women ages 30 to 55. This is a synthesis of the six dedicated reports only. It is not a market-share, ranking, demand, clinical-efficacy, or feature-parity analysis.

## Status resolution and source hierarchy

1. **Youper:** Its dedicated report’s official homepage is controlling. Services ended September 30, 2026. Do not characterize residual pages as live product pages.
2. **Woebot:** The dedicated Woebot report is controlling over incidental Woebot references in the other five reports. The official FAQ is the primary availability source: consumer app retired June 30, 2025; no new or prior-account access. The report also notes that older referral and access-code pages conflict with that FAQ and do **not** verify an active partner program or purchasable offering.
3. **Ash is separate from Woebot and Youper.** The Ash report identifies Slingshot AI as the product owner; no approved report establishes a rebrand, affiliation or succession relationship.

## Editorial read-through

The strongest recurring pattern is not a broad absence of stress, sleep, menopause or caregiving content. Headspace, Calm and Wysa all have meaningful public coverage of those components. The usable opportunity is a high-specificity, safety-bounded update to an existing StressGuru route: a work-to-evening transition, post-caregiving workday handoff, private workplace script, or sleep-related escalation boundary. Ash’s public blog has a narrower observed public surface, but that does not change the need to validate an audience need before editing.

No source report measures search volume, keyword difficulty, rankings, traffic, conversion, clinical outcomes, AI-answer-engine citations or product superiority. Those fields remain unmeasured, not zero.

## Three differentiated editorial opportunities

These are recommendations to improve existing routes, not claims of unmet demand or approvals to publish. They deliberately avoid claims that five AI sources or any untested benchmark supports an outcome.

1. **“Hard night to workday” caregiver handoff.** Improve the existing caregiving-and-work guide with a phone-first triage, one neutral workload-prioritization message and a benefits-resource prompt that tells readers to confirm policy with HR. Calm publicly covers caregiver fatigue, sleep and job pressure, but its dedicated report did not identify a sampled public page that packages the first workday after an overnight disruption into a timed, privacy-safe plan. Headspace’s reported caregiver material is similarly brief rather than a specific adult-caregiver work-and-sleep workflow. [Calm report](./calm.md#2-the-employee-caregivers-overnight-disruption-to-workday-handoff) [Headspace report](./headspace.md#narrow-evidence-linked-content-gap-hypotheses)

2. **Private “night waking to next meeting” support.** Improve the verified perimenopause sleep-and-work route with a non-diagnostic nighttime option, morning cognitive-load triage, optional neutral focus-protection wording and a clinician-discussion boundary. Calm and Headspace cover menopause, sleep and work-related components, while the respective reports describe the integrated, privacy-preserving next-meeting format as a hypothesis rather than an established public page. [Calm report](./calm.md#1-a-discreet-perimenopause-workday-reset-after-disrupted-sleep) [Headspace report](./headspace.md#1-question-can-stressguruai-create-a-clinician-reviewed-guide-for-professional-women-in-midlife-on-managing-a-night-of-disrupted-sleep-when-perimenopause-symptoms-and-next-day-work-demands-collide-including-a-decision-path-for-when-to-seek-clinical-care)

3. **Post-conflict “park it until tomorrow” script.** Improve the existing conversation-rumination page with a fact-versus-interpretation prompt, an optional next-day repair sentence and a deliberate sleep-protective stopping point. Wysa’s report recognizes its existing work-conflict, mindfulness and sleep support, so the differentiator is the narrow post-conflict-to-bedtime format, not a claim of superior capability. Headspace’s work and sleep resources likewise are reported as separate public components. [Wysa report](./wysa.md#3-a-private-script-for-sleep-disrupting-work-rumination-after-conflict) [Headspace report](./headspace.md#3-question-can-stressguruai-make-the-work-to-night-transition-more-actionable-for-women-whose-work-pressure-unfinished-tasks-or-difficult-interactions-turn-into-bedtime-rumination)
