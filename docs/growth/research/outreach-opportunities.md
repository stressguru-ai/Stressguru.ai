## Guardrails before any future send

- Obtain explicit outreach approval first. This document is not authorization to contact an outlet.
- Use only a verified organizational editorial contact path. Do not infer or create a private reporter email.
- Do not present an AI wellness persona as a clinician, spokesperson, therapist or source of medical advice.
- Do not promise outcomes, cite unmeasured benchmark results, imply a partnership or endorsement, or offer disease-specific support.
- Do not treat media mentions as citations, rankings or evidence of product quality.
- If an outlet requests product, pricing, privacy, clinical or safety details, route the response for review under the approved policy.
